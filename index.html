Chapter 1 · Before Dawn

The alarm rang at 4 a.m. The trailhead was cold, dark and silent. You tightened your laces, switched on your headlamp, and took the first step toward a summit you couldn't even see.

Chapter 2 · The Forest

Into the pines

The trees closed in around you, tall and patient. Every step was roots and needles and the sound of your own breathing. Then the light found its way through, and the forest opened.

Chapter 3 · The Ridge

1,200 m

The Switchbacks

Forty-two hairpin turns. You stopped counting at nineteen and started counting breaths instead.

2,100 m

The Treeline

The forest thinned, then vanished. For the first time, you could see how far you'd come.

2,900 m

The Scramble

Hands on cold rock. Slow, careful, deliberate. The wind picked up and so did your heartbeat.

3,600 m

The False Summit

You thought it was the top. It wasn't. You sat down, ate an orange, and stood back up.

4,392 m

The Last Push

Every step felt like three. But the sky was getting bigger, and the ground was running out.
